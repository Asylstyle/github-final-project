# Simple Interest Calculator

A calculator that calculates simple interest given the principal
amount, annual interest rate, and time period in years.

## Inputs

- p: principal amount
- r: annual interest rate as a percentage
- t: time period in years

## Output

The simple interest earned over the specified time period.

## Formula

Simple interest = (p * r * t) / 100

## Example

Principal amount: 1000
Annual interest rate: 5%
Time period: 2 years

Simple interest = (1000 * 5 * 2) / 100 = 100
